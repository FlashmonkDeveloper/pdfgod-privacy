Privacy Policy

PDF GOD · Last updated November 7, 2026

Your PDFs, scans and photos are processed on your phone and are never uploaded. The free version shows video ads from Google AdMob.

The short version

  • Your PDFs, scans and photos are processed on your phone. They are never uploaded.
  • There is no PDF GOD account, and we run no servers that receive your files.
  • The free version shows video ads from Google AdMob, and those ads use the internet and collect device and ad data.
  • We (the developer) never receive, see or store your files.

Your files

When you open a file, PDF GOD reads it on your phone to do what you asked. Results are saved as new files in Downloads/PDF GOD, and your original stays as it is. Temporary working copies are kept in the app's private storage and are cleared automatically.

What PDF GOD stores on your phone

All of this stays in the app's private storage:

  • Settings such as your app colour, Home page choices and app lock setting
  • Your recent files, reading position and bookmarks
  • The signature you save, if you choose to save one
  • The search index for Search all PDFs, which holds text from PDFs you have opened or made and from folders you add. You can delete it in Settings under Clear search data.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this data.

Advertising

The free version unlocks tools with a video you choose to watch. Those videos come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Your files and their contents are never given to the ad service.

Google's handling of this data is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Other Google services in the app

  • Text and barcode recognition (ML Kit): recognition runs on your phone, and your images and text are not sent anywhere. Google's ML Kit may send anonymous usage and diagnostic information about how the feature performs.
  • Google Play: on copies installed from Google Play, the app asks Play whether an update is available and may show Play's rating dialog. Play handles these under Google's own terms.

Pro plans

Pro plans are not on sale yet. When they are, payment will be handled by Google Play. We will not receive your card or payment details, and this policy will be updated first.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Your files only leave your phone when you choose to share or save them somewhere yourself, for example with the Share button. Printing goes through your phone's print service.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling PDF GOD deletes everything in its private storage. Files you saved in Downloads/PDF GOD are ordinary files that you can delete yourself.

Limits worth knowing

  • Password protection is only as strong as the password you choose.
  • A signature made in PDF GOD is a drawn mark, not a certificate-based digital signature.

Children

PDF GOD is not directed at children under 13.
